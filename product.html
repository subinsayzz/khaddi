Questions

Is this pashmina or cashmere?
Both words describe the same fibre. European law asks us to write “100% cashmere” on the composition label, so we do. Khadi-Pashmina is our name for the process: spun and woven entirely by hand.
Will I pay customs when it arrives?
No. Every price includes duty and VAT, and the courier is paid before the parcel leaves India.
Why does it take a week to make?
Because pashmina fibre is too fine for a machine unless it is blended with nylon and then washed in acid. We spin and weave it as it is.
What is the tag in the hem?
An NFC chip. Hold a phone to it and you see who made your piece. It can be removed without harming the cloth.
