How a Nāma piece is made

01

Pick the cloth

Any stole or shawl in the shop, or one from the next drop.

02

Write the line

We send a drawn proof in both scripts within a day. Nothing is stitched until you say yes.

03

Embroidered

By hand, in the hub in Basohli. The embroiderer’s name goes on the passport.

04

Filmed and sent

Forty seconds of your name being stitched, then a numbered box to your door.

Before you order

Personalised orders cannot be returned.

EU rules give fourteen days to change your mind, but not on something made to your specification. We will always send the proof first, and we will re-embroider at cost if you ever want it changed.
