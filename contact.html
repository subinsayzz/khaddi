Before you write

When will my order arrive?
In-stock pieces ship in 48 hours and take about seven days to the EU. The date is shown before you pay.
Can I visit the hub?
Yes, by arrangement. Write to us with your dates.
Do you discount?
No. The price is what the work costs, and the makers are paid before anyone else.
Can you repair a piece?
Yes. Book through the Thread Passport and it goes back to the person who made it.
